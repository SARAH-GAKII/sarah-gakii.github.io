## Overview

This project analyzed customer behavior, pricing dynamics, and operational efficiency within an online food delivery platform. Using a real-world transactional dataset, it examines how order value, cuisine type, delivery logistics, and customer feedback influence platform revenue and service performance.

*Completed as part of a supervised academic program.*

## Data & Tools

Real-world food delivery orders dataset, analyzed in Python with pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

## Methodology

- Data cleaning, missing-value handling, and exploratory and multivariate analysis of pricing distributions, demand concentration, delivery and preparation times, and cuisine-level trends
- Correlation analysis and segmentation to relate operational metrics to customer behavior
- A rule-based revenue model in Python estimating platform earnings, with recommendations on staffing, promotions, and revenue optimization

![FoodHub analytics overview](/images/work/foodhub-thumbnail.png)

## Key Insights

- High-value orders contributed a disproportionate share of platform revenue despite representing a minority of total orders.
- Delivery time variability, rather than food preparation time, was the primary operational bottleneck.
- Customer ratings skewed heavily toward high values, with many orders left unrated — feedback did not fully represent all customer experiences.
- Weekend demand consistently exceeded weekday demand, pointing to opportunities for targeted staffing and promotions.

## Outcome

This project demonstrates how Python-based analytics can translate raw operational data into actionable insight for revenue optimization, logistics planning, and customer engagement.
