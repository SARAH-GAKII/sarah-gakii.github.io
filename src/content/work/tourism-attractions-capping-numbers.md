## Context

At the Mathematics in Industry Study Group (MISG 2023), a team worked on a problem brought forward by an industry representative: how to determine fair and effective visitor caps for tourism attractions — an open-ended challenge with direct implications for resource management, sustainability, and economic planning.

Tourism attractions face tension between maximizing revenue and preserving visitor experience and environmental sustainability. Setting a cap on visitor numbers is a practical necessity, but determining that cap requires balancing attraction size, environmental impact, peak demand, and operating costs.

## Contribution

- Translated the industry question into a mathematical modeling problem
- Worked through assumptions and constraints — visitor flow, carrying capacity, cost-benefit trade-offs
- Explored initial model formulations aimed at capturing attraction-use dynamics to guide optimal capping strategies

## Reflection

The project strengthened skills in problem formulation for open-ended, real-world contexts, team-based research under tight deadlines, and communicating technical approaches to both academic and industry audiences.
