## African Mathematical Modeling Union (AMU) School - Nairobi, Kenya (July, 2023)

Worked collaboratively on mathematical models of crime dynamics during an intensive modeling school bringing together researchers and students from across Africa. The project examined how cooperation between policing units could alter predicted crime outcomes. Presented the work at multiple stages during the program, including the final project report.


## Africa Scientifique Workshop - AIMS South Africa (Feb. & June 2023)

Developed and presented a community outreach proposal focused on financial literacy, exploring how mathematical and scientific training could be translated into practical community engagement. The workshop brought together participants across disciplines to develop initiatives connecting science, education, and sustainable development.

## Mathematical Modeling of Biological Systems - AIMS South Africa (Mar. 2023)

Delivered a presentation on Mathematical Modeling of Biological Systems during a three-week Mathematical Biology course block at AIMS, exploring how mathematical models can be used to represent, study, and predict complex biological systems.

The presentation examined the choices involved in building useful biological models, including the tradeoff between simple and complex representations and the distinction between deterministic and stochastic approaches. Biological examples illustrated how additional mechanisms can increase model realism while also increasing complexity.

## Mathematics in Industry Study Group (MISG) - South Africa (Jan. 2023)

Worked in collaborative teams on open-ended mathematical problems during a two-week intensive study group, developing and presenting work throughout the modeling process.

- **Green Roofs to Mitigate the Urban Heat Island:** analyzed a heat-transfer model for green-roof substrates, incorporating solar irradiance, convection, radiation, and evapotranspiration. Applied non-dimensionalization, Laplace transforms, and finite-difference methods to obtain analytical and numerical solutions and investigate how vegetation can influence heat absorption, storage, and release. Co-presented the final project report.

- **Tourism Attractions Capping Numbers:** worked on an industry-proposed problem concerning fair and sustainable visitor limits for tourism attractions. Explored mathematical formulations balancing environmental impact, visitor experience, carrying capacity, and economic considerations.

## Mfano Africa - Oxford Mathematics Virtual Mentorship Programme (2022)

Completed a research mentorship project under Dr. Robert A. McDonald of the University of Oxford examining competition between red and grey squirrel populations through a dynamical-systems model. Developed and simulated the population model in MATLAB, analyzed how initial population conditions affected long-term outcomes, and considered how stochasticity could extend the deterministic model. Presented the work during the programme's final symposium.
